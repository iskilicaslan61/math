# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students in grades 6 and 7 use the site to study mathematics and complete weekly practice. Teachers manage student access, assign a week's learning materials, and review submitted work.

## Product Purpose

The product provides curriculum-aligned explanations, practice tests, games, and printable assessments. Its weekly workflow lets teachers release one week at a time and lets students submit that week's work after completing all ten tests.

## Brand Personality

Clear, encouraging, dependable. The interface should help students understand what to do next and give teachers a reliable view of access and progress.

## Anti-references

Avoid interfaces that expose every week's work to students at once, obscure access or submission status, or use distracting decoration that competes with learning.

## Design Principles

- Make the student's next available learning task obvious.
- Show weekly progress and submission state where students and teachers need them.
- Keep teacher controls explicit and protect against assigning the wrong grade or week.
- Use plain, age-appropriate Turkish and consistent interaction patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for text contrast, keyboard access, and form labeling. Keep instructions readable for middle-school students and respect reduced-motion preferences.
